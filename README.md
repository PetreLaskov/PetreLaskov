# Petre Laskov

I investigate questions and make useful things with AI: tools, working procedures, source-linked knowledge and explanations.

My background is in leading quantitative and qualitative research. For roughly three years, practical and intellectual work with LLMs has been my main focus. Working fluently with agents has widened the range of problems I can take on. I define the purpose and requirements, learn what the task demands, direct development and review the result. Agents contribute substantial research, writing, code and testing.

## Selected work

- [Cyborgism / Personal AI OS](https://github.com/PetreLaskov/personal-ai-os) — records for decisions, disagreements and sources, so a later session can recover the work and its reasons.
- [Harness Engine](https://github.com/PetreLaskov/harness-engine) — document-based interviews and working-knowledge records that feed generated AI instructions. Includes a runnable demonstration of structural checks.
- [Writing and visual work](https://petrelaskov.github.io/) — essays, selected AI-assisted visual work and other forms of inquiry. [Deutsche Welle published a Macedonian version of my air-pollution essay](https://p.dw.com/p/2qkw7) in January 2018.

I am also developing hands-on ways for people to work with AI. I am interested in research, practical integration, explanations and collaborations that connect a worthwhile question to something people can use.
